AI Video Generation

Cinematic, on-brand video - from brief to broadcast.

Built on Seedance, a leading text-to-video and image-to-video model, our pipeline produces far more video, far faster, at a dramatically lower cost per asset - without giving up creative control or brand safety.

How the pipeline works

Five stages take a brief to broadcast-ready cuts.

1

Brief and script

Concept development, storyboarding, and prompt design that translate a creative idea into a generation plan. You stay in the loop on direction from the first hour.

2

Style frames

Brand-locked look development, reference imagery, and visual guidelines, so every output is consistent with your identity.

3

Seedance generation

Text-to-video and image-to-video generation, produced in batches, with cinematic camera and motion control. Start from a script, or bring existing stills, product shots, and characters to life.

4

Edit and sound

Professional editing, colour grading, music, voiceover, and captioning - the craft that turns raw generation into a finished film.

5

Deliver

Multi-format, per-channel export ready for publishing - the same concept adapted across placements, markets, and languages.
