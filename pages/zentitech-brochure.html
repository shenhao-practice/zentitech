Who we are

One team across strategy, creative, and build.

Zentitech is an AI studio and consultancy that helps organisations move from AI ambition to measurable, production-grade results. Where most partners either advise without building, or build without a strategy, we do both. We bring together three disciplines that rarely sit under one roof - creative production, strategic consulting, and software engineering - so a strategy is grounded in what can be built, a video looks intentional and on-brand, and a tool is production-ready rather than a fragile demo.

We work in short, outcome-based cycles. Every phase ends with something tangible you can review, use, and decide on. There are no long lock-ins and no black boxes: you own the code, prompts, pipelines, and playbooks we produce.
