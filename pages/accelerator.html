AI Accelerator

One win becomes an AI-native organisation.

The accelerator packages strategy, delivery, and creative into a fast, repeatable programme. It leaves behind reusable assets, playbooks, and enablement that make AI a standing capability, not a one-off project.

How it works

A programme designed to build momentum, then hand it to your team.

1

Baseline and pick the first wins

We assess readiness, agree the ambition, and select a small set of high-value, high-feasibility use cases to lead with.

2

Ship a lighthouse use case

We deliver the first use case to production - proof that is real, visible, and worth building on.

3

Build reusable foundations

Prompt libraries, agent templates, pipelines, and connectors are turned into shared building blocks the next use case can reuse.

4

Enable your team

Upskilling, playbooks, and a standing centre of excellence, so your people can carry the work forward with confidence.

5

Scale and compound

Each new use case ships faster than the last as the platform and patterns mature. We step back as your capability steps up.

What is inside

The accelerator draws on all three practices at once.

Reusable building blocks

Prompt libraries, agent templates, and pipelines you keep, extend, and reuse across teams.

Centre of excellence

A lightweight operating model and the governance to run AI safely at scale.

Enablement

Hands-on upskilling and playbooks that leave your people able to build, not just brief.

What you are left with

The point of the accelerator is that you do not need us forever.

  • A live, production use case delivering value
  • Reusable assets that speed up the next build
  • A team trained to build and operate AI
  • A governance model that keeps it safe
  • A roadmap of sequenced next use cases
  • Full ownership - no black boxes, no lock-in
